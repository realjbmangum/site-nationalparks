5 Best Parks for First-Timers

With 63 parks to choose from, deciding where to start can feel overwhelming. Don't overthink it. These five parks are famous for a reason -- they deliver jaw-dropping scenery, well-maintained trails, and enough infrastructure that you won't feel lost on your first visit.

1. Great Smoky Mountains -- The most visited park in America, and it's completely free. Cades Cove, Clingmans Dome, and miles of easy waterfall hikes make this the gentlest introduction to the park system.

2. Yellowstone -- Old Faithful, Grand Prismatic Spring, and wildlife everywhere. The Grand Loop Road hits every highlight in one drive. Nothing else in the world looks like this place.

3. Grand Canyon -- Stand on the South Rim and your brain genuinely cannot process the scale. The Rim Trail is flat, paved, and gives you 13 miles of views without breaking a sweat.

4. Yosemite -- Half Dome, El Capitan, Bridalveil Fall. The valley floor is accessible by free shuttle, and every direction you look is a postcard.

5. Zion -- The Narrows and Angels Landing are bucket-list hikes, but even the Pa'rus Trail along the Virgin River is stunning and wheelchair-accessible.

Quick Tip

Great Smoky Mountains is the only park on this list with no entrance fee. It's the perfect zero-cost trial run before you invest in the America the Beautiful Pass.
