The Most Beautiful Drive in America Connects Two National Parks

There's a road trip that starts in Shenandoah National Park, follows the spine of the Appalachian Mountains for 469 miles along the Blue Ridge Parkway, and ends in Great Smoky Mountains National Park. And here's the best part -- the entire route is free. No tolls, no entrance fees on the parkway itself.

Start at Shenandoah's Skyline Drive, where 75 overlooks give you views of the Shenandoah Valley that will stop you mid-sentence. Hike Dark Hollow Falls (1.4 miles round trip, easy) before you head south.

The Blue Ridge Parkway picks up where Skyline Drive ends. Stop at Mabry Mill (milepost 176) for the most photographed spot on the entire parkway. Detour to Linville Falls (milepost 316) for a gorge hike that rewards you with three separate waterfall views.

Finish in the Great Smoky Mountains, where Clingmans Dome gives you a 360-degree panorama of mist-covered peaks. Hit Cades Cove early morning for wildlife -- black bears, deer, and wild turkeys are regulars.

Road Trip Tip

Plan 4-5 days minimum for this drive. The Blue Ridge Parkway speed limit is 45 mph and there are hundreds of pulloffs worth stopping at. Rushing this road defeats the purpose. Book lodging in Asheville, NC at the midpoint for a rest day.
