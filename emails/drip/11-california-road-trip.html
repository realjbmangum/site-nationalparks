The California Big 3: One Loop, Three Legendary Parks

There is a road trip in California that connects three of the most dramatically different landscapes in America -- and you can drive it in a single loop from either LA or San Francisco.

Start at Yosemite, where Half Dome and El Capitan tower over the valley floor. Hike the Mist Trail to Vernal Fall, or just sit in a meadow and stare at waterfalls. Two days here feels right.

Then head south to Sequoia. Standing at the base of General Sherman -- the largest tree on Earth by volume -- resets your sense of scale entirely. Walk the Congress Trail to see dozens of ancient giants in a single mile.

Finally, drop east into Death Valley. Badwater Basin sits 282 feet below sea level. Zabriskie Point at sunrise looks like another planet. The contrast from giant sequoias to the driest place in North America is staggering.

The full loop is about 800 miles and works perfectly in 7-10 days. Spring and fall are ideal -- Death Valley gets dangerously hot in summer.

Quick Tip

Book Yosemite lodging 3-5 months ahead. For budget savings, stay in Oakhurst or Mariposa outside the park and drive in each morning -- you'll save $100+ per night.
