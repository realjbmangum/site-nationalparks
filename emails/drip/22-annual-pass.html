This $80 Pass Saves You Hundreds on Park Visits

If you're planning to visit more than one or two national parks this year, the America the Beautiful Annual Pass is the single best investment you can make. Here's why.

The pass costs $80 and covers entrance fees at all 63 national parks plus 2,000+ federal recreation sites -- national forests, wildlife refuges, BLM lands, everything. It covers a vehicle and all passengers, and it's valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

Let's do the math. Yellowstone charges $35 per vehicle. Grand Canyon is $35. Yosemite is $35. Zion is $35. Visit just three of those parks and you've spent $105 without the pass -- or $80 total with it. Every park after that is free for the rest of the year.

Even a modest road trip hitting Rocky Mountain ($30), Grand Teton ($35), and Yellowstone ($35) would cost $100 at the gate. The pass saves you $20 on that single trip and keeps saving all year.

Money-Saving Tip

Buy the pass at your first park entrance station -- no need to order online. Active military and their dependents get the pass free. Seniors 62+ can get a lifetime pass for $80 (one-time). 4th graders get a free annual pass through the Every Kid Outdoors program.
