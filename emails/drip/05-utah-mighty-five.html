The Ultimate Utah Road Trip: 5 Parks in 10 Days

Utah is the only state with five national parks, and they're all within driving distance of each other. The "Mighty Five" loop is one of the greatest road trips in America, and you can do it in 10 days without feeling rushed.

Here's the route:

Days 1-2: Zion -- Start with The Narrows, a river hike through a 1,000-foot-deep slot canyon. If you're up for it, Angels Landing is a chain-assisted climb with views that will haunt your dreams (in a good way). Permit required.

Days 3-4: Bryce Canyon -- Just 80 miles from Zion. Sunrise at Bryce Point overlooking thousands of orange hoodoos is one of the most surreal sights on the planet. Hike the Navajo Loop and Queen's Garden trails.

Day 5: Capitol Reef -- The least-visited of the five, and maybe the most underrated. Drive the Scenic Drive, pick free fruit in the Fruita orchards (seriously), and hike Hickman Bridge.

Days 6-7: Canyonlands -- Vast, remote, and humbling. Island in the Sky district has Mesa Arch at sunrise -- the most photographed arch in Utah. Grand View Point Overlook will make you feel very, very small.

Days 8-9: Arches -- Over 2,000 natural stone arches, including the iconic Delicate Arch. Time the 3-mile roundtrip hike to Delicate Arch for sunset. You won't regret it.

Day 10: Drive back to Las Vegas or Salt Lake City. Spend the drive reliving the best 10 days of your life.

Quick Tip

Fly into Las Vegas and drive 2.5 hours to Zion to start the loop. Rental cars are cheaper in Vegas, and you'll end in Moab near Arches -- an easy drive back to SLC or back to Vegas via I-70.
