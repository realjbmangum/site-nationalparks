How Many Parks Have You Checked Off?

There is something deeply satisfying about tracking which national parks you have visited. Maybe you have been to 3. Maybe 15. Maybe you are one of those people quietly working toward all 63. Whatever your number is, it deserves to be celebrated.

That is why we created our National Parks Badge Sticker Collection. Each park has its own uniquely designed sticker inspired by the classic WPA travel poster style -- bold colors, iconic landmarks, vintage lettering. They look great on water bottles, laptops, journals, or a dedicated park map where you mark off each one as you go.

The collection is not just decorative, though. It is genuinely motivating. There is something about seeing a blank spot on your map or a sticker you have not earned yet that makes you think, "Okay, maybe Arches is next." Before you know it, you are planning your next trip.

Some of our subscribers have started with a handful and now they are building road trips specifically to fill in gaps on their collection. One couple told us they planned an entire Southwest trip around the five parks they were missing from the Utah Mighty Five set.

Whether you are just starting your park journey or you are deep into the quest for all 63, the collection is a fun way to remember where you have been and get excited about where you are going next.

Quick Tip

Pair your sticker collection with the National Park Passport book (sold at most park visitor centers for about $13). Each park has a unique cancellation stamp you can ink into the book as a permanent record of your visit -- date included.
